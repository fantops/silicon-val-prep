## 4. Decision read

1. **Intel is the financial and strategic anchor** — higher base + cash LTRCA reduce dependence on equity timing; the conservative 5-yr band is a solid cash floor.
2. **The real Intel upside is career compounding** — Intel BLR validation = 3-5 year specialization engine: PCIe/CXL, power, pre-Si methodology, debug depth. That improves odds for AMD/hyperscaler/silicon exits later.
3. **Qualcomm is a real but still incomplete counter-option** — the 2 Jun loop tested PM-flow ownership and hardware-adjacent C reflexes. Relevant, but no written offer / level / team scope exists yet.
4. **AMD is the most interesting career-upside gate** — R2 clarified the role as CPU core post-silicon fixed-frequency performance, with power expansion and pipeline-level / multi-IP debug. Could compound faster than generic validation if the team actually owns core perf debug.
5. **The honest caution on AMD** — also the highest skill-gap risk right now: benchmark literacy, fixed-frequency perf methodology, TDP framing, and CPU pipeline counter-debug vocabulary are not yet strong. Do not over-rank it before written scope and manager expectations are clear.

### 4A. Personal decision note

Intel is not risk-free, so use the conservative band for planning. Even then, Intel gives a strong cash floor, clear domain alignment, and a better story for the next move. The rational position: join Intel, keep spending conservative for the first 12 months, and reassess only if a clearly superior silicon role appears with written TC + stronger scope.
